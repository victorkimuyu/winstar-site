Turnstiles, Bollards & Barriers

We control pedestrian and vehicle entry while keeping your premises moving.

Different entrances.
The right control.

Winstar designs and installs access controls around the way your premises work. From busy staff entrances to residential driveways and loading areas, we help you manage entry without disrupting daily activity.

01

Turnstiles & controlled entrance for visitors

We install turnstiles and pedestrian gates to help you control staff and visitor entry. We integrate compatible card or biometric readers and provide wider passage options for wheelchair users and deliveries.

02

Bollards

Our bollard solutions restrict vehicle access to pedestrian areas, building frontages and designated zones. We match fixed, removable or retractable options to your site, keeping restricted areas separate while allowing authorised access where needed.

03

Vehicle barriers

We install vehicle barriers that put your team in control of car parks, estate entrances and loading areas. We select equipment for your lane width and traffic, with guard-operated or compatible automated access controls.

More than a barrier.
A managed entrance.

Winstar brings equipment, technology and people together to strengthen entry control. We connect compatible barriers and gates with card or biometric access, CCTV and guarding procedures, giving your team a coordinated way to manage arrivals.

Plan around your traffic

We assess your busiest periods, visitor checks and deliveries, then plan for queues and separate pedestrian routes where space allows.

Install for everyday use

Our team checks your site, power supply and existing systems before installation. We test the equipment and show your staff how to operate it.

Keep access dependable

We help you plan servicing, backup power and emergency access, explain manual-release procedures, and review your setup as your needs change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we connect our existing readers?+

We check the reader, controller and entry equipment together to establish compatibility and identify any changes needed.

What happens during a power failure?+

Operation depends on your equipment and backup supply. We plan suitable backup or alternative access arrangements with you and show your staff how to use the manual-release procedure where applicable.

Does every bollard provide impact protection?+

No. Traffic-control bollards do not automatically provide impact protection. If vehicle impact is a concern, we address it separately when assessing your requirements and selecting appropriately rated equipment.

What do you need to prepare a quote?+

Share the site location, entrance dimensions, types of users and vehicles, and your busiest periods. Photos or an existing layout can help us understand the entry point.
