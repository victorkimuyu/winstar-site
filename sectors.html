Who We Serve

We shape security around how your premises work, from busy airport terminals and hotel entrances to schools, homes and warehouses.

Banking & Financial

A branch needs to feel open to customers while keeping staff areas controlled. We help banks and SACCOs plan a visible security presence, support staff during busy periods and agree how alarms and incidents should be handled.

Customer areas
Provide a visible guard presence at entrances and help manage queues when the branch is busy.
Staff-only access
Limit entry to offices and back-of-house areas to authorised staff and approved visitors.
Alarms and incident review
Agree how panic alerts are handled and use CCTV in appropriate public areas to support follow-up.

Hotel & Recreation

Security is part of the guest experience. At hotels, resorts and leisure venues, we coordinate with reception and venue staff so security teams can help with arrivals, keep service areas controlled and adjust coverage for busy events.

Guest arrivals
Help guests find reception and manage vehicle movement at entrances and drop-off areas.
Events and functions
Agree entry checks, queue arrangements and staff contact points before guests arrive.
Grounds and service areas
Plan patrols for car parks and grounds, and agree how staff and deliveries enter service areas.

Real Estate & Domestic

Residents need to come and go easily, while property managers need a clear way to handle visitors, contractors and deliveries. We shape coverage around the property, from a single home to a shared estate or managed building.

Visitors and deliveries
Agree how guests and contractors are admitted and who can approve access.
Shared spaces
Plan guard patrols and appropriate camera coverage for entrances, parking and common routes.
Boundaries and alerts
Assess gates, fencing and alarms together, including who receives alerts when the premises are unattended.

Education

A school gate has different demands at opening time, during lessons and at the end of the day. We help schools and campuses plan coverage around those routines and agree how guards work with staff and follow the institution’s safeguarding procedures.

Pick-up and drop-off
Support agreed arrival and collection procedures and help keep pedestrian routes clear at peak times.
Visitors and contractors
Guide visitors to reception, confirm who is expecting them and follow the institution’s sign-in process.
After-hours coverage
Plan access and patrols around evening classes, campus events and periods when buildings are closed.

Aviation & Travel

Terminals need to keep passengers moving while maintaining clear boundaries around restricted areas. We work with aviation operators to define each post, match staffing to expected passenger activity and agree how concerns are handed over.

Designated access points
Check permissions at agreed posts and refer access issues through the operator’s procedures.
Passenger movement
Guide passengers near screening areas and refer screening concerns to the responsible team.
Shift handovers
Confirm duties, report incidents and keep the next shift informed as passenger volumes change.

Logistics & Warehousing

At a warehouse or cargo yard, security has to keep pace with dispatch. We plan gate coverage, yard patrols and surveillance around the flow of drivers, staff and goods so checks fit the operation.

Vehicle arrivals and departures
Use agreed procedures to record drivers, vehicles and arrivals at the gate.
Goods and loading areas
Monitor goods moving through loading bays and refer discrepancies to the site team.
Closing and dispatch
Plan yard and perimeter patrols outside operating hours and assess escort support for selected movements.
