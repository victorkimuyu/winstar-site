About Winstar

Professional security in Kenya since 2001, with trained people on site and a team behind them around the clock.

Winstar Security Solutions protects businesses, institutions and homes across Kenya. Our head office in Mombasa works with regional teams in Nairobi, Nakuru, Kisumu and Eldoret to support the people we deploy.

A hotel needs a welcoming presence at reception. A warehouse needs careful checks at the gate. We agree with you what matters at your site, prepare our team for those duties and give you an account contact who knows the assignment.

Behind every guard on duty

Training before deployment
Our guards complete a three-week programme covering access control, customer care, drill and safety. They learn how to check an entrance and how to handle a difficult conversation there.
Supervision in the field
Our supervisors check in with officers on site. When routines change or a concern needs attention, your account team helps translate that into clear instructions for the next shift.
A 24-hour control room
Our operators keep in radio contact with guards and coordinate mobile response when an alarm comes in. The officer at your gate has someone to call, day or night.

A dependable partner, now and into the future

Our Managing Director and senior team set priorities and allocate resources across the business. Operations managers coordinate our regional teams, HR and administration look after our people, and our ICT team safeguards the records and communications those teams rely on.

Our business continuity plan sets out who makes decisions during a disruption, which functions take priority and how we restore normal operations. It covers staffing, equipment, communications and the recovery of critical records. The plan calls for a review at least once a year and whenever a significant incident or organisational change gives us reason to revise it.

Regulatory Compliance

Private security licence
Winstar holds a licence from Kenya’s Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA) to provide private security services.
Industry membership
Winstar is a member of the Kenya Security Industry Association (KSIA), the professional association for our industry.
Data processor registration
We hold a valid Data Processor Certificate from the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. If an assignment involves visitor records or CCTV footage, you can ask our team about the handling requirements for your site.
