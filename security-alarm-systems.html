Alarm Systems, Monitoring & Response

We install intruder and panic alarms backed by control-room monitoring and rapid response.

Alarms for intrusions, emergencies and perimeter breaches

01

Intruder alarms

We install door contacts, motion detectors and vibration sensors around your vulnerable areas to detect unauthorised entry, including while your premises are unattended.

02

Panic alarms

We provide fixed or wireless panic buttons that connect residents, staff and guards to our control room when they need assistance.

03

Connected perimeter protection

We integrate compatible electric fencing and CCTV with your alarms, configuring fence activations and selected camera events to trigger alerts.

Monitoring backed
by rapid response.

Our control room monitors alarm signals around the clock, connects each alert to the right premises and coordinates the response. Mobile teams attend to assess the situation, supported by clear communication with the control room and your nominated contacts.

  1. Alarm activated

    A detector or panic button raises the alert.

  2. Signal received

    The control room identifies the site and handles the activation.

  3. Team dispatched

    We dispatch personnel under your site’s response procedure.

  4. Site assessed

    The team checks the premises and coordinates any further action.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you monitor or upgrade an existing alarm?+

We assess the equipment, condition and communication setup before recommending reconnection, additions or replacement. Your existing system may provide a useful starting point.

What happens during a power cut?+

Backup batteries support continued operation, with runtime depending on the system and battery condition. Suitable communication links allow alarm signalling without relying solely on your Wi-Fi.

How are false activations handled?+

Our control room handles activations according to your site’s response instructions. Correct sensor placement, regular checks and proper use help reduce nuisance alarms.

Is rapid response available in my area?+

Share your location with us so we can advise on local response coverage. Your quote will set out the installation, monitoring and response services for your premises.
