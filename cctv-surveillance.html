Frequently Asked Questions

Can CCTV eliminate every blind spot?+

We position cameras to reduce blind spots, but obstacles can block views. We review coverage as your site changes.

Will the cameras work at night?+

We match cameras to your night-time viewing distance. Infrared works within its illumination range; colour footage needs visible light.

Does AI mean every incident is detected?+

No. Analytics can miss or misclassify activity. Good positioning, lighting, configuration and human review remain essential.

What happens if the internet or power goes off?+

Local recording can continue without internet if cameras, recording equipment and the local network remain powered and connected. Remote access may stop; backup power needs suitable capacity.

How long can footage be kept?+

We size storage around your retention needs, camera count and recording settings, taking your data-handling requirements into account.

Can you improve an existing CCTV system?+

Yes. We assess reusable equipment, coverage gaps and compatibility before recommending upgrades.
