VIP & Cargo Escorts

We protect people and cargo on the move with planned, coordinated escorts.

Different journeys.
Focused protection.

Winstar provides escorts for individuals and sensitive cargo. We assess your itinerary, destinations and security concerns, then match personnel and coordination arrangements to the assignment.

VIP & personal protection

We provide close protection for VIPs during visits, meetings and events. Our team checks venues in advance and coordinates arrivals and departures, with personnel trained in VIP protection, de-escalation, emergency procedures and first aid.

Cargo escorts

We escort sensitive cargo from origin to destination, coordinating with your transport team throughout the journey. Our escort vehicles and personnel use tracking technology, with real-time monitoring through Winstar’s 24-hour control room.

Prepared for the journey.
Connected along the way.

We agree the scope before deployment, including movement plans, key contacts and reporting arrangements. Clear communication helps our teams address changes and concerns without losing sight of your schedule.

Advance preparation

We review routes, timings and destinations, identify security concerns and agree responsibilities with your team before the assignment begins.

Coordinated movement

Our personnel maintain contact with the relevant client, driver and security teams, reporting progress and escalating concerns through agreed channels.

Arrival & handover

We coordinate arrival with your nominated contact and confirm completion of the escort, recording incidents or follow-up needs where applicable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work with our existing transporter?+

Yes. We plan the escort with your transport team and agree driver contacts, timings and destination arrangements. The assignment focuses on security support for your cargo movement.

Can we arrange protection for a single visit?+

We can plan an escort for a specific visit, event or journey. Share your dates and requirements so we can assess the assignment and confirm availability.

Which routes can you cover?+

We assess your proposed route, destinations and schedule before confirming coverage and deployment arrangements. Discuss changes with our team so we can review the plan.

What should we share when requesting a quote?+

Start with the escort type, dates, general locations and duration. Our team will arrange a direct discussion of detailed itineraries, cargo information and security concerns.
