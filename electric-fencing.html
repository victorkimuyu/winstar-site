Electric Fencing

We strengthen your perimeter with fencing that deters intrusion and supports early warning.

Fencing for your boundary

We survey your walls, gates and grounds before recommending a fence. For an existing installation, we check what can be repaired or upgraded.

Wall-top fencing

We install electric fencing above suitable perimeter walls to discourage climbing. We assess wall condition, corners and gate openings to plan coverage around your property.

Freestanding fencing

For suitable sites without a perimeter wall, we design freestanding electric fencing around the terrain, boundary layout and movement nearby.

Repairs & upgrades

We inspect existing fences, trace faults and repair damaged components. We assess the energiser, wiring and alarm connections before recommending repairs or upgrades.

Effective deterrent with connected response

We connect compatible fencing to alarm monitoring and response, so fence activations can reach our control room. We agree how our teams investigate alerts and coordinate with your on-site security.

Planned & installed

We select the energiser and fence components for your perimeter, plan gate connections and warning signs, then test the installation before handover.

Power & alerts

We plan suitable battery backup and configure supported fault and tamper alerts, helping your team recognise when the fence needs attention.

Serviced & supported

We check wires, insulators, connections and battery condition. We also flag vegetation or damage that could affect performance and explain routine operating checks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you fit a fence to an existing wall?+

Yes, where the wall is suitable. We assess its condition, height, gate openings and nearby activity before recommending the installation.

Will the fence work during a power cut?+

A suitable backup battery can keep the energiser running. Runtime depends on the equipment, battery condition and fence load. We assess backup needs during the survey.

Can you repair a fence installed by another provider?+

We assess the existing equipment and faults first, then recommend repairs or replacement where components are damaged, incompatible or no longer serviceable.

Does the fence replace guards or CCTV?+

No. It adds deterrence and detection at the boundary. We can combine it with guarding, CCTV and an agreed response service for broader protection.
