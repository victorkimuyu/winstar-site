Aviation Security

Security personnel for airport access points, screening areas and passenger-facing posts.

Security that keeps passengers moving

Our officers combine attentive security with calm, clear communication.

Gates & entry points

We staff designated posts and verify entry permissions under your site procedures.

Screening-area support

We guide passengers through queues and refer concerns to the responsible screening team.

Terminal assistance

We help passengers and staff with directions, monitor assigned areas and report incidents.

Working with your aviation team

Before deployment, we agree post duties, shift coverage and reporting lines with your team. On duty, supervisors coordinate handovers and make sure concerns reach the right airport contact.

Operating screening equipment is agreed separately and depends on training and authorisation.
