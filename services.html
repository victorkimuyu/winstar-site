Choosing your security.

A few answers to help you explore your options. For advice about your premises, speak to our team.

Where should I start when choosing security services?+

Tell us about your premises, operating hours, site users and concerns. We use these details to recommend a suitable combination of personnel, systems and perimeter protection.

Can guarding and electronic security work together?+

Yes. We combine on-site guarding with cameras, access systems and alarms to improve visibility and control. We plan how these services work together around your layout and daily operations.

What is the difference between electronic access and physical access control?+

Biometric and card-based systems help determine who is authorised to enter. Turnstiles, bollards and barriers manage physical movement through an entrance. These measures can complement one another, which is why you will find them in separate groups on this page.

What information should I provide when requesting a quote?+

Include your location, property or business type, the services you are interested in and your preferred start date. If known, add the operating hours, number of entrances and any existing security arrangements. Avoid sharing passwords, access codes or other sensitive security details through a general enquiry.
