Planning your
guarding service.

A practical starting point for discussing duties, personnel and support.

What duties can a Winstar guard undertake?+

Our guards manage access, maintain occurrence books and incident reports, assist visitors and respond to incidents. We agree their responsibilities with you to suit your premises.

How are guards selected and prepared?+

We vet and interview candidates, then train them in practical guarding, access control, customer care, public relations, and health, safety and environmental awareness.

How are guards supported while on duty?+

We support guards with supervisory patrols and radio contact through our control room. Our teams document activity in occurrence books, incident reports and daily summaries.

Can guarding be arranged for day and night coverage?+

Yes. We provide round-the-clock guarding, agreeing staffing, shifts, duties and support with you as part of your service scope.

What should I share when requesting a guarding quote?+

Tell us the site location, type of premises, operating hours, entrances to be covered and your preferred start date. Explain any visitor or delivery requirements. Detailed security procedures can be discussed directly with the team.
