Event Security

We protect your guests and venue, from first arrivals through final departures.

Prepared before arrival.
Vigilant until end of event.

We work with your organisers and venue team to agree responsibilities, communication and incident procedures. Our supervisors brief personnel on their posts, guest handling and when to escalate concerns.

Before

We review the layout, schedule, entry points and security risks, then agree staffing, screening and coordination with the venue’s emergency arrangements.

During

We supervise posts, monitor activity and respond to concerns, coordinating with organisers, venue staff and designated emergency services when needed.

After

We support orderly departures and can extend guarding through equipment removal or overnight storage, with hours and responsibilities agreed in advance.

A welcoming, vigilant presence.

Winstar provides security for corporate functions, private celebrations and public gatherings. We plan around your venue, programme and expected attendance, combining courteous guest assistance with clear access controls.

Guest entry & screening

We check invitations, tickets or credentials and carry out agreed entry screening. Our team helps guests find the right entrance while restricting unauthorised access.

Queues & crowd movement

We guide arrivals, monitor busy areas and help keep exits and walkways clear. Our team flags congestion and coordinates with organisers as activity changes.

Restricted areas & equipment

We control access to backstage, staff and storage areas, helping protect equipment and supplies. We agree access arrangements for performers, suppliers and authorised personnel.

Parking & vehicle access

We manage entry to designated parking and service areas, guide vehicles within the venue and help keep delivery routes separate from guest arrivals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many security personnel will we need?+

We assess attendance, venue layout, entrances, programme and risks before recommending staffing. Guest numbers alone do not determine the right team.

Can you work with our venue’s security team?+

Yes. We agree posts, responsibilities and reporting lines with your venue and organisers so our teams work together throughout the event.

Can you cover setup and overnight equipment?+

We can include setup, overnight guarding and pack-down in your assignment. Share your supplier schedule and storage arrangements when requesting a quote.

What information do you need for a quote?+

Send your event date, venue, expected attendance and programme, including setup and finish times. Mention ticketing, restricted areas and any particular security concerns.
