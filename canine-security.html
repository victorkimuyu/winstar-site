K9 Security Dogs

Our trained dogs and handlers provide patrols, visible deterrence and specialist detection.

Patrol and specialist detection

We match the dog and handler to what you need, whether that’s regular site patrols or screening for a specific assignment.

Patrol dogs

Our teams provide a visible presence at gates and around site boundaries. The dogs help handlers pick up signs of unusual activity when visibility is limited.

Warehouses, logistics yards, construction sites and residential estates

Specialist detection dogs

Our specialist teams screen for specific scents, including explosives or narcotics. Handlers interpret the dog’s signals and coordinate with your security team under agreed procedures.

Event venues, shopping centres and suitable aviation facilities

Every dog works
with a trained handler

Our handlers direct each dog, interpret its signals and communicate with your security team. We plan routes and working hours around your staff, residents and visitors.

We provide medical care, proper nutrition and purpose-built transport for our dogs. We plan deployments with water, rest and suitable working conditions.

How we plan your deployment

  1. Assess the site

    We review risks, boundaries and how people move through your property.

  2. Agree the assignment

    We agree where and when the team will work, taking other site users into account.

  3. Coordinate with security

    We set reporting procedures and connect our handlers with your guards or control room.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a dog team work with our existing guards?+

Yes. Our K9 teams reinforce patrols alongside your guards. We agree routes, responsibilities and communication arrangements with your team before deployment.

Are patrol and detection dogs interchangeable?+

The roles require different training. Patrol teams provide a visible presence and help identify unusual activity. Specialist detection teams search for the scents they are trained to recognise.

Can K9 teams work where people are present?+

It depends on the task, layout and movement of people. We plan where our handlers can safely work with their dogs.

What information do you need for a quote?+

Share your location, site layout, hours of operation and the areas you need covered. Let us know whether you need routine patrols or a specific screening assignment.
